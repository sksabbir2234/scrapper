Job Search Tips

1
Tailor every application — customize your resume for each job. Use keywords from the description. Generic resumes get rejected by ATS.
2
Follow up — send a polite email 5-7 days after applying. Most people skip this.
3
Network actively — 70% of jobs are never posted publicly. Connect on LinkedIn.
4
Track applications — use a spreadsheet: company, role, date, status, follow-up.
